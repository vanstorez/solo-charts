Method & guardrails

  • Signals: EMA 20/50 with a 200-EMA regime filter; RSI(2) < 10 dip buys above the 200-SMA, exit on RSI > 60 or a close above the 5-SMA; 20-day Donchian breakout with a 10-day low exit.
  • Fills: decided on a close, executed at the next open. No same-bar or look-ahead fills.
  • Costs: 0.15% per side, approximating brokerage, STT and slippage for NSE delivery trades. Intraday costs and taxes are higher.
  • Data: TradingView scanner for live quotes/ratings; Yahoo Finance adjusted daily OHLC for history. Adjusted prices include dividends.
  • Reality check: survivorship bias is present because the universe is today's large caps. Position sizing is 100% of capital per symbol, which is aggressive.
  • Next: add a walk-forward test, cap position size, and paper-trade before risking capital. This is research tooling, not investment advice.
